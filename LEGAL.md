# Legal & App Store Compliance — Quenly

## Regulated Medical Device Status

**Declaration: No — this app is not a regulated medical device.**

Quenly is a general wellness / personal health tracking application. It:

- Does not diagnose, treat, mitigate, or prevent disease
- Does not make clinical decisions or recommendations that replace a clinician
- Does not interface with medical hardware as a controlled device
- Provides only user-entered logging, local charts, and rule-based category labels based on publicly available AHA/ACC guideline ranges

Use this answer in App Store Connect when asked for regulated medical device status (US / UK / EEA).

## Privacy Nutrition Label (Apple)

Answer **Data Not Collected** for all categories.

Rationale: The app has no backend, no analytics, no account system, and never transmits health or personal data off the device.

## Age Rating (App Store Connect)

Recommended approach:

- Medical/Treatment Information: select carefully. Prefer “Infrequent/Mild” if the questionnaire requires it, because the app shows blood-pressure category labels. Avoid “Frequent/Intense”.
- No unrestricted web access, no gambling, no mature content.
- Target age rating: 4+ or 12+ depending on the final questionnaire answers. Do **not** claim the app is for clinical use.

## Required in-app statements

The app must (and does) display:

1. Clear medical disclaimer that it is not a medical device and not a substitute for professional care.
2. Link to this Privacy Policy.
3. Statement that data never leaves the device.

## Google Play Data Safety

- Data collected: None
- Data shared: None
- Data encrypted in transit: N/A (no network transmission of health data)
- Users can request deletion: Yes (Clear All Data in Settings)
